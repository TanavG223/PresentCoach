ONE LOCAL FLASK PIPELINE

Measurements first.
Language model second.

01

Watch and listen

MediaPipe analyzes facial landmarks at 15+ FPS while a native local microphone stream captures your practice.

02

Build the evidence

whisper.cpp creates word timestamps. Python calculates pace windows, filler clusters, gaze breaks, movement, and quality flags.

03

Replay every claim

The Flask review workspace pairs encrypted local video with seekable measured events and clearly labeled session-wide notes. Ollama can prioritize verified findings, but cannot invent a metric, timestamp, or judgment.

TRUST GATE

Direct coaching. Measured evidence.

A valid 30+ second recording receives actionable feedback using disclosed general practice bands. One reviewed baseline and two matched repeats add personal-reference comparisons.
