ONE LOCAL PIPELINE

Measurements first.
Language model second.

01

Watch and listen

MediaPipe analyzes facial landmarks at 15+ FPS while a native local microphone stream captures your practice.

02

Build the evidence

whisper.cpp creates word timestamps. Python calculates pace windows, filler clusters, gaze breaks, movement, and quality flags.

03

Verify every claim

Ollama proposes feedback, but Python rejects or removes anything without an allowed metric, exact value, and timestamp.

TRUST GATE

No invented benchmarks.

PresentCoach does not ship a universal definition of a “good speaker.” It asks you to inspect one baseline and complete two matched repeat recordings before AI feedback unlocks.

  1. 1Record and review a 30+ second baseline
  2. 2Capture two nearly identical repeats
  3. 3Unlock feedback only after repeatability passes

PRIVATE BY ARCHITECTURE

Your practice stays yours.

↯

No cloud inference

Camera, audio, Whisper, metrics, and Ollama execute on your Mac.

◇

No raw-media archive

Raw audio is discarded after transcription. Camera frames are analyzed in memory.

⌑

Encrypted history

Session records use authenticated encryption and a random key stored in macOS Keychain.

✓

Adversarially evaluated

30/30 synthetic cases passed, including missing data, excellent sessions, short recordings, and appearance bait.
